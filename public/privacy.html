Akhlaq privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026. This policy describes the Akhlaq standalone Android app and its optional browser/Termux version.

Your records stay with you

Akhlaq has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. Your prayer log, reflections, dhikr counts, saved verses and hadiths, chosen routine, preferences and listening position are stored on your device. The Android app disables automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup for its private data.

Location and compass

If you choose “Use my location”, Android or your browser asks for location permission. Akhlaq uses your coordinates locally to calculate prayer times and Qibla. Akhlaq does not send these coordinates to its developer or audio providers, and does not request background location. You can choose a city or enter coordinates instead and revoke permission in device settings. Android’s location service is also subject to your device provider’s settings and policies.

Compass sensor readings are processed on the device while the compass is active and are not uploaded.

Quran text and audio connections

Selected hadiths, daily practice ideas and Prophet story summaries are bundled for offline reading. Browsing Learn does not contact a content provider. Opening an external source or correction link connects to that website only when you choose it.

The Android app includes Arabic, English and Urdu Quran text. Reading, search and prayer calculations work without internet. In the browser/Termux version, choosing Urdu downloads its edition from QuranEnc.

When you stream or download recordings, your device connects directly over HTTPS to the provider for the selected language: Islamic Network / Al Quran Cloud (cdn.islamic.network, Arabic), QuranEnc (d.quranenc.com, English), or EveryAyah (everyayah.com, Urdu). Those providers receive your IP address, the recording URL identifying the requested verse, request timing and technical connection information. Their own policies govern any server logs and retention. Akhlaq does not control or promise deletion of provider logs. Offline playback of a fully downloaded recording does not need a provider connection.

Notifications, downloads and files

Android foreground services keep audio playing in the background and handle downloads you request. Notifications show playback controls or download progress. These services do not provide prayer alarms. You can stop playback or pause downloads inside the app. Downloads consume storage and may use mobile data.

Practice backups and prayer calendars are written only after you choose an export location. A backup can contain private reflections and prayer records. Your selected file or cloud-storage provider controls the exported copy. Restoring a backup reads only the file you select; Akhlaq does not request access to all your files.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it, clear app storage or uninstall. In Android, Settings → About, privacy & help → Erase my data deletes the app’s local records and audio downloads and closes the app after confirmation. Quran → Offline audio → Remove audio downloads removes recordings without erasing your practice. In a browser, clear this site’s storage to remove local records. Exported backups are separate files; delete them yourself from their saved location. No account-deletion request is needed because Akhlaq has no accounts.

External links and support

Source and support links open external websites, which have their own privacy policies. Support is available through the Akhlaq project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include private reflections, precise coordinates, credentials or other sensitive information.

Changes

Changes to app data practices will be reflected here with a revised effective date.
