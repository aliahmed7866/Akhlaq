          Improve the reading
          
        1. Remove magnetic cases. Move away from metal, speakers and chargers.
        2. Slowly trace a figure eight with your phone.
        3. Hold it flat, screen up. Tap Recheck and turn slowly.
        
          
        A steady reading can still be wrong near interference. GPS finds your position; the phone sensor finds your direction. Compare with a trusted compass or the direction at your local prayer space.

        Prayer alerts & adhan

        Android alerts use your saved location and calculation settings. They do not need Termux or an open screen.
        



        

        Alerts are prepared for 90 days and refreshed when you open the app. Reopen after travelling or changing your clock. Android notification, sound, battery and Do Not Disturb settings can affect delivery. Force-stopping prevents alerts until reopening.
        

        The adhan is a 27-second spoken reading by Jarih, unchanged, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0. It is not a melodic recording or a Fajr-specific version; Android may shorten notification sounds.
        

        Reading comfort
        Quran text also has its own size controls. Your preference stays on this device.
        
      
        
        Back up your practice
        
        The file includes your prayer log, dhikr counts and private reflections. Restoring fills missing days and keeps entries already on this device.
