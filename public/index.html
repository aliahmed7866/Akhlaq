    Sources, editions & accuracy

    Arabic and translations are reproduced unchanged from HadeethEnc's official downloads, including publisher explanations and grading. Each selected teaching has a cross-checked Bukhari reference. Some publisher records combine parallel narrations; they are not presented as verbatim text from one Bukhari edition.

    Source checking is not a guarantee that human translations or editorial summaries are error-free. No independent scholarly review of this app has been completed. Consult a qualified scholar for personal religious rulings.

    HadeethEnc permits free reuse under its conditions: preserve content, credit the publisher, retain version and transcript information, report corrections, keep editions updated, and avoid inappropriate advertising. Publisher and reuse terms ↗

        About, privacy & help
        
        Akhlaq · Prayer, Quran and daily practice
        
        
        No account, ads or analytics. Prayer times and Qibla are calculated on your device. Quran text is included in the Android app; streaming and audio downloads need internet.
        
        
        For background listening, press Play, then lock your screen. Use the Android media notification to pause or resume. Force stop, restart or phone battery restrictions can stop playback.
